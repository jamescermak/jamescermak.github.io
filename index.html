Fluid Solver VFX Add-on

This is a custom fluid solver I developed in Python as an add-on for DCCs. It computes Navier-Stokes equations for fluid dynamics on a cell-based grid resulting in fluid textures on meshes.

Shader Animation

Inspired by Beauty and the Beast. My goal was to drive various shader properties procedurally. This resulted in creating various masks (emission, noisy mesh destruction, transparency) that respond to the 3D position of controllers within the scene.

Geometric Music Visualizer

This project explored how animation, emission, and shaders can be procedurally driven by audio. I specified many different frequencies to direct light brightness/timing, the movement of the assets, and the direction and warping of textures/shaders.
